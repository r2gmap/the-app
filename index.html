How rewards actually work

Every reward is tied to a task, and every payout is traceable.

  • Rewards are tied to tasks

    Each offer states its requirement and its exact reward before you start.

  • You submit proof

    Finish the requirement, then upload a screenshot showing it is done.

  • A person reviews it

    Submissions are checked manually by an administrator, not by an automated tracker.

  • Approved rewards reach your wallet

    An approved submission creates a wallet transaction you can trace back to the offer.

  • Rejections come with a reason

    If a submission is rejected, you receive the reason in the app and can try again.
