About The App

We turn time spent in games, apps and websites into rewards that are actually paid out.

Why we exist

Rewards platforms can be difficult to evaluate when requirements and payout status are unclear. The App keeps the catalog focused, the requirements explicit, and the process easy to follow from start to finish.

Every opportunity is published with a clear requirement and reward. Submissions are verified before rewards are issued, with status visible throughout.

  1. 01

    Transparency first

    The requirement, the reward and the review status are always visible. No hidden conditions.

  2. 02

    Verified before rewards

    Submissions are verified before rewards are issued, keeping the process fair and transparent.

  3. 03

    Rewards that arrive

    Approved rewards land in your wallet and are paid out to your own cash wallet on request.
