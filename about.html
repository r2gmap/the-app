About The App

We turn time spent in games, apps and websites into rewards that are actually paid out.

Why we exist

Rewards platforms have a trust problem. Too many promise easy money and disappear the moment a payout is due. We built The App to be the opposite: a small catalog of real opportunities, clear requirements, and a payout process you can follow end to end.

Every opportunity is published by our team with an exact requirement and an exact reward. When you submit proof, a person on our verification team reviews it — not a black box, not a bot farm.

  1. 1

    Transparency first

    The requirement, the reward and the review status are always visible. No hidden conditions.

  2. 2

    Verified by people

    Every submission is reviewed by our verification team to ensure a fair and transparent reward process.

  3. 3

    Rewards that arrive

    Approved rewards land in your wallet and are paid out to your own cash wallet on request.
