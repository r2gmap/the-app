Frequently asked questions

How long does verification take?

Most submissions are reviewed within 48 hours. Complex ones can take a little longer — you will always see the current status on your submissions page.

Why was my submission rejected?

The proof did not clearly show the requirement was met. The reviewer's reason is shown with the decision, so you can fix it and try again.

How do I receive my money?

Once a reward is approved it lands in your wallet. Request a withdrawal and the amount is sent to the cash wallet number saved on your profile.

Is it free to join?

Yes. Creating an account and participating is completely free — we never ask for a payment.
