Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information The App collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. Plain language, no surprises.

Information we collect

When you create an account we store your email address, display name and profile picture. When you participate we store your submissions — including any proof images you upload — and the cash wallet number you provide for payouts.

How we use it

Your information is used to run the platform: verifying your submissions, paying out approved rewards, and keeping your account secure. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Google Firebase. Proof images are kept only as long as needed for verification and dispute resolution.

Your choices

You can edit your profile details at any time, and you may request deletion of your account by contacting support.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open a private support ticket and our team will help.
